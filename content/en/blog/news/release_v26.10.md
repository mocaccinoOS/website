### Changes since 26.09

- Kernel upgraded to 6.18.57 (LTS)
- GCC version 16.2.1
- Mesa version 26.2.4
- KDE Plasma is upgraded to version 6.7.5
- COSMIC 1.10.0
- Nvidia-drivers version 595.104.02 (available in repository)

### Issues and improvements

- Still testing optional [x86-64-v3 optimized](https://www.mocaccino.org/blog/2026/08/05/new-optional-x86-64-v3-builds-zero-risk-for-everyone-else/) builds for improved performance on supported modern CPUs.

### Project sustainability note

MocaccinoOS is currently supported by community donations totaling **approximately $1 per month**.
While development continues, this level of funding is not sufficient to sustainably support long-term work.

If you find MocaccinoOS useful and would like to help keep the project healthy, consider supporting us via donations.

https://www.mocaccino.org/docs/donate/

### Acknowledgement
MocaccinoOS builds on the incredible work of the Gentoo Linux community. We are grateful for their efforts, and all packages are compiled on our own infrastructure powered by Kairos

### Available editions

MocaccinoOS v26.10 is available in five desktop editions: KDE Plasma, XFCE, GNOME, MATE, and COSMIC.

Whether you prefer a modern, feature-rich desktop, a lightweight environment, a traditional layout, or a fresh and innovative desktop experience, there’s an edition to suit your workflow.

All editions share the same MocaccinoOS foundation, giving you a consistent approach to software management and system updates, with the freedom to choose the desktop experience that works best for you.
